Home / FAQ

Questions, answered.

A few things people ask before their first visit. Anything else, just call the shop.

Do I need an appointment?

Appointments are best so we can give your dog our full attention. Quick nail trims are often welcome as walk ins when the schedule allows. Call ahead to check.

What are your hours?

Hours are confirmed with the shop before launch, as public listings currently disagree.

Where are you located?

12388 Garden Grove Blvd, Garden Grove, CA 92843. See the map on our Visit page.

What services do you offer?

Baths, full grooms, nail trims, de shedding, and gentle care for ears and teeth.

Do you groom all breeds and sizes?

Yes. From tiny toy breeds to big doubles coats, tell us the breed when you book and we will plan the right time and price.

How much is a groom?

Pricing depends on your dog's size, coat, and the style you choose. Call the shop and we will give you a friendly quote.

Still have a question?

Call the shop and we will give you a straight answer.
